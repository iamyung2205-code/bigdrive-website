FAQs

Good to know

How do I book a seat?

Pick a route from the departure board, choose your travel date and time, add any extra luggage, and enter your details. You'll get a booking reference and payment instructions.

How do I confirm my payment went through?

After you transfer and tap "I've sent the transfer," your booking is saved as pending. Big Drive verifies the payment against your reference and updates your ticket to confirmed — you can check the status anytime under "Track your booking."

Can I change my travel date after booking?

Reach out to Big Drive directly on WhatsApp with your booking reference and we'll help sort out any changes.

How much luggage can I bring?

Two free bags per passenger are included. Extra or fragile items can be added for a small fee during booking.
